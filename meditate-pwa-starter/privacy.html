Privacy Policy

Equipoise respects your privacy. We do not collect any personal data. All meditation stats, preferences, and history are stored locally on your device only.

Audio files may be cached on your device for offline use. You can manage this from within the app.

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts required. Your meditation practice is yours alone.

If you contact us by email, your message will be used only to respond.

Made with calm in Switzerland.
